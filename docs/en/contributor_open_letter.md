# An Open Letter to Developers

"Spring waters warm first to the ducks, just as the blowfish is about to swim upstream!"

Dear friend, I am John, an ordinary Java programmer working in a company. Today, I am writing this open letter with sincere feelings to you, who love AI technology and innovation.

Six months ago I saw many excellent projects, such as `Dify`, `Chat2DB`, and other AI-related projects. I thought, how wonderful it would be to be able to participate in these projects. Unfortunately, I can't find a way to contribute and have spent ten years writing code in vain.

In early 2025, I came across a video by the Xiege team, which piqued my curiosity about how they accomplished it. I wanted to replicate their backend services to create a low-cost civilian Jarvis. Unfortunately, the works I've created so far are still just artificial idiots - they have low concurrency, no soul, slow response, and many bugs.

The Xiege team is our learning model. I really want to have an intelligent Xiaozhi backend service like the Xiege team. But I also understand the decision of not open-sourcing. "A single flower does not make spring; a garden full of flowers makes spring." The era of AI blooming everywhere may be realized by us in this generation. We can achieve a low-cost civilian Jarvis with our own hands. I personally believe that if he can do it, we can also do it, it's just a matter of time, which I call "our journey to obtain scriptures."

What difficulties will we encounter on this journey to obtain scriptures? I think there should be no less than eighty-one hardships. On this journey, various monsters will certainly appear, but there will also be gods secretly helping us, and people will join our team on this journey.

If you think the above content is funny, then I consider myself very fortunate. I can make you laugh for five seconds in your life of more than 30,000 days, which is also a contribution to you.

Will the idea of civilian low-cost Jarvis fail? I don't know, but in our ordinary lives, this kind of failure is not uncommon, right?

In the future, there is one thing that can be certain: someone will surely completely replicate the functions of the Xiege team and realize a civilian low-cost Jarvis. Will this project be us?

Looking forward to working hand in hand with you to create the future.

John, March 11, 2025, Guangzhou

# Appendix: Development Contribution Guidelines

## Project Goals

1. **Civilian Low-Cost Jarvis Solution**

2. **Intelligent Linkage Solution for Peripheral Hardware**

## Join Us

We warmly welcome like-minded friends to join us and contribute to the project together. You can view the functions we plan to implement recently at [this link](https://github.com/users/xinnan-tech/projects/3). The functional list that has not yet been assigned to anyone is what we urgently need your participation in. The participation methods are as follows:

### 1. Become a Regular Contributor

Fork the project, submit a PR, and after review by developers, it will be merged into the main branch.

### 2. Become a Developer

After you have cumulatively submitted 3 valid PRs, you can contact the group owner to apply to become a developer. The group owner will invite you to join an independent developer group to discuss the future of the project together.

## Developer Development Process

1. **Create New Branch**
   Each functional point should be developed in a new branch. The branch name should be concise and clear, so that one can immediately see the function being implemented, avoiding functional conflicts.

2. **Submit PR for Review**
   After completing the functional development, please submit a PR on GitHub for review by other developers. Once reviewed and approved, it will be merged into the main branch.
